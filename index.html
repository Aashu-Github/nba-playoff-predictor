02 — Methodology

How it works.

The project follows a conventional ML pipeline: collect historical playoff data, engineer matchup-level features, train with a temporal split, evaluate, and then use the model to score future matchups.

01

Data collection

Pull historical playoff logs.

The pipeline uses nba_api to collect game and team performance data across roughly ten NBA seasons.

nba_apiPythonHistorical playoffs
02

Preprocessing

Turn team stats into matchup differentials.

Home-minus-away features convert two team profiles into one row the classifier can evaluate consistently.

PandasFeature engineeringRolling form
03

Training

Train without leaking future seasons.

A temporal holdout keeps later playoff seasons out of training so evaluation better reflects real prediction conditions.

Logistic Regressionscikit-learnTemporal split
04

Evaluation + inference

Measure discrimination, then score matchups.

ROC analysis and other model checks are used before the trained artifact is loaded for matchup-level probability predictions.

ROC AUCjoblibProbability output

03 — Features

What the model sees.

The model uses matchup differentials instead of treating each team in isolation. Positive or negative values capture the relative advantage entering a game.

DIFF_OFF_RTG_PROXY

Offensive Rating

Estimated points produced per 100 possessions, represented as the home-team advantage over the away team.

Very high
DIFF_DEF_RTG_PROXY

Defensive Rating

Estimated points allowed per 100 possessions, helping quantify defensive strength in a playoff matchup.

Very high
DIFF_REST_DAYS

Rest-Day Advantage

Home-team rest minus away-team rest, capturing scheduling and fatigue differences.

High
DIFF_ROLL_PLUS_MINUS

Rolling Plus-Minus

Recent net scoring performance designed to capture team form entering the matchup.

Medium-high
DIFF_EFG_PCT

Effective Field Goal %

Shooting efficiency adjusted for the extra value of three-point field goals.

Medium
DIFF_TOV_RATE

Turnover Rate

Relative possession loss rate, an important signal in close playoff games where each possession matters.

Medium
DIFF_FG3_PCT

Three-Point %

Relative three-point shooting efficiency between the home and away teams.

Supporting
DIFF_PACE_PROXY

Pace

Relative possession tempo, helping describe stylistic differences between opponents.

Supporting
